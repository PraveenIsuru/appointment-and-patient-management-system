How to use the system

Step-by-step instructions for the tasks your role can perform

Signing in and out

  1. Enter the username and password issued to you and select Sign in.
  2. Tick Keep me signed in only on a device that is yours. It keeps you signed in for 14 days.
  3. Select Sign out in the top bar when you finish. On a shared clinic computer, always sign out rather than only closing the window.
  4. If the message Invalid username or password appears, check both fields. The same message is shown whichever one is wrong, deliberately: it stops anyone using the form to discover which usernames exist.

Administrator tasks

  1. Open Dashboard from the top bar.
  2. Registering an appointment, searching by appointment number, and issuing bills become available in the next milestones.
  3. Give each member of staff their own account. Shared accounts make the audit trail meaningless, because every change then looks like the same person.

Dentist tasks

  1. Open My schedule to see your consulting session and your appointments for the day.
  2. You see only your own schedule and the histories of patients assigned to you.

Patient tasks

  1. Open My appointments to see your record and upcoming visits.
  2. Keep your contact number up to date so the clinic can reach you if a appointment has to be moved.
  3. You can see only your own records. Nobody else's are reachable from your account.

Keeping the clinic's data safe

  • Never share your password. Clinic staff will never ask you for it.
  • Patient records are health data. Open only the records you need for the task in front of you.
  • Every change to an appointment is recorded in the audit trail with the username that made it and the time it happened.
